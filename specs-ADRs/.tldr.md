# specs-ADRs

## Scope

Architecture decision records: why each design decision was made. Organized by lifecycle stage. How this folder works is specified in [specs/adr-structure.md](../specs/adr-structure.md) and [specs/adr-process.md](../specs/adr-process.md). Only archive/ and rejected/ are indexed; proposed/ and candidates/ hold work-in-progress files that are opened directly when the user references them.

## Contents

- [archive/](archive/.tldr.md) — adopted ADRs (updating specs, implementing, implemented, incl. superseded), their permanent home, grouped by year and month. Linkable.
- [candidates/](candidates/) — undecided ideas, temporary until turned into a proposed ADR or dropped. Work in progress, never linked. TLDR disabled.
- [rejected/](rejected/.tldr.md) — rejected ADRs and what failed, permanent home, flat. Linkable.
- [proposed/](proposed/) — drafted ADRs being worked toward acceptance, with tentative names. Work in progress, never linked. TLDR disabled.
