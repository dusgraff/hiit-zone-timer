# heart-rate-HIIT

## Scope

Root of the Heart Rate HIIT Pacer: a browser-based (Android Chrome) HIIT pacing tool driven by a Polar heart rate monitor. Holds project context, specs, and the app itself as they're added.

## Contents

- [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) — project overview: platform (Android browser, Polar HR over Bluetooth) the workout model (warm-up, HR-gated exercise cycles), and that specs/ is authoritative with design changes going through the ADR process.
- [specs/](specs/.tldr.md) — authoritative living specs, starting from README.md. So far: the ADR structure and process; app specs not yet written.
- [specs-ADRs/](specs-ADRs/.tldr.md) — architecture decision records (why decisions were made), by lifecycle stage: candidates, proposed, archive.
